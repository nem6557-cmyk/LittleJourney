Delete Your Account

LittleJourney

Delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open LittleJourney and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Download My Data.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete My Account, then confirm.

You can download a copy of your data first using the same screen.

Can't sign in? Ask us to delete it

Email privacy@littlejourney.app from the address on your account with the subject “Account deletion request”. We may ask you to verify your identity, and we will complete verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, sign-in credentials and push notification token
  • Messages you sent, comments, reactions and notifications
  • Your links to children, classrooms and conversations
  • Your avatar and any other files you uploaded to your profile
  • Any active subscription (cancelled) and saved payment cards (removed)

What may be kept

  • Daycare-owned records (attendance, incident reports, milestones and activity entries about a child). If you were a caregiver, these are kept without your name.
  • Invoices and payment records held by Stripe and by your daycare, which are retained for tax and financial record-keeping.
  • Encrypted backups, which are overwritten on their normal rotation schedule.

Daycare administrators

If you are the only administrator, deleting your account cancels the daycare's subscription. Invite another administrator first if the daycare should keep using LittleJourney.
