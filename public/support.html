Contact

For product support, email support@littlejourney.app.

For privacy requests, email privacy@littlejourney.app.

For legal notices, email legal@littlejourney.app.

Before You Write

  • Include the email address on your LittleJourney account.
  • Include your daycare name if the issue is tied to a daycare record.
  • Do not include sensitive child health details in the first email unless support asks for them.

Data Requests

You can start export and deletion requests inside the app from Profile > Privacy > Data Management. Support may need to verify your identity before completing privacy or account requests.
