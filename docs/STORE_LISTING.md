### Description (4000 chars max)

Little Journey Daycare bridges the gap between parents and childcare providers with real-time updates, secure messaging, and a living journal of your child's day.

FOR PARENTS:
- Receive real-time activity updates throughout the day — meals, naps, diaper changes, learning activities, and outdoor play
- View a beautiful timeline of your child's day with photos and caregiver notes
- Track developmental milestones and celebrate achievements
- Communicate securely with your child's caregivers through in-app messaging
- Receive daily summary reports with everything that happened
- Manage attendance check-in and check-out from your phone
- Access your child's profile, allergies, medical notes, and emergency contacts in one place

FOR DAYCARE CENTERS:
- Log activities quickly with an intuitive caregiver interface
- Capture and share photos with families in real time
- Send messages to individual parents or broadcast to entire classrooms
- Manage classrooms, staff, and enrollment from the admin dashboard
- Track attendance digitally — no more paper sign-in sheets
- Generate invoices and manage tuition payments through Stripe integration
- Onboard new families with a simple invite code system

PLANS & PRICING:

- Daycare Professional: $59/month — up to 50 children, 5 classrooms
- Daycare Enterprise: $99/month — unlimited children and classrooms
- All daycare plans include a free 14-day trial

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
- COPPA and FERPA compliant
- Row-level security ensures complete data isolation between organizations
- End-to-end encrypted authentication
- No data sold to third parties — ever
- Parents can export or delete their data at any time

## Privacy Policy (Web-Hosted Version)

Host this at: https://littlejourney.app/privacy

### Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 2026**

#### 1. Information We Collect

Little Journey Daycare ("we", "our", "us") collects information necessary to provide childcare communication services:

**Account Information:** Name, email address, and role (parent, caregiver, or administrator).

**Child Information:** Name, date of birth, classroom assignment, allergies, medical notes, and emergency contacts. This information is entered by parents and authorized caregivers only.

**Activity Data:** Meals, naps, diaper changes, learning activities, outdoor play logs, milestones, and attendance records entered by caregivers.

**Photos:** Images uploaded by caregivers or parents related to childcare activities.

**Messages:** Communications between parents and caregivers within the app.

**Payment Information:** Processed securely through Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers on our servers.

**Device Information:** Device type, operating system version, and app version for technical support and crash reporting (via Sentry).

#### 2. COPPA Compliance

Little Journey Daycare is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

- We do not collect information directly from children under 13
- All child data is entered by parents, guardians, or authorized caregivers
- Parental consent is required before any child data is collected
- Parents can review, delete, or refuse further collection of their child's data at any time through their account settings

#### 3. FERPA Compliance

Educational records are protected under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA):

- Little Journey Daycare acts as a school official with legitimate educational interest
- Access to educational records is strictly limited to authorized daycare staff and the child's parents or guardians
- Educational records are not shared with unauthorized third parties

#### 4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide real-time activity updates to parents
- Enable secure communication between parents and caregivers
- Generate daily reports and milestone tracking
- Process payments and manage subscriptions
- Send essential notifications (activity updates, messages)
- Improve service reliability and performance (crash reporting)

#### 5. Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security policies ensuring multi-tenant data isolation
- Authentication tokens are stored using device-level encryption (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android)
- Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3
- Access is controlled through role-based permissions at the database level

#### 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared only with:

- **Your daycare center** (as authorized by enrollment)
- **Stripe** for payment processing
- **Sentry** for crash reporting (anonymized device data only)
- **Supabase** as our cloud infrastructure provider

#### 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- You can request complete data export or deletion at any time through Profile > Privacy > Data Management
- Upon deletion request, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days
- Backup data is purged within 90 days of deletion

#### 8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccuracies in your data
- Request deletion of your data
- Export your data in machine-readable format
- Withdraw consent for data collection
- Opt out of non-essential communications

#### 9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of material changes to this privacy policy via in-app notification and email at least 30 days before changes take effect.

#### 10. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries: privacy@littlejourney.app
For general support: support@littlejourney.app
For legal matters: legal@littlejourney.app
